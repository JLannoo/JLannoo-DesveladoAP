# Desvelado

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) contains all the options you need to configure and export a
config file.

## What is DesveladoAP?

Desvelado is a room-based precision platformer in which you control Vampy, a little vampire getting ready for bed by
turning off all the lights in his castle.

***What about the AP part?***  
DesveladoAP is a randomizer where some of the game's basic mechanics are taken away from you, and you have to find them
along the way.

The mechanics include:
- **Key Switches**: Switches that toggle blocks in the room. While locked, they are intangible.
- **Ghost Switches**: Switches that toggle barriers for ghosts. Same as above.
- **Falling Platforms**: Platforms that crumble when you touch them. While locked, they stay intact, blocking progress.
- **Ghost Walls**: Blocks that can only be passed through by dashing. While locked, they stop you in your tracks.
- **Chandeliers**: Protected lights that can only be touched after breaking their globe by dashing through it. While
  locked, they are intangible.

Levels are also randomized: each level's **Map Part** is shuffled into the item pool, and a level only becomes
available once you have the map parts of the levels that lead to it.

## What counts as checks/locations?
Checks in Desvelado come in 3 forms:
- Clearing a room
- Picking up one of Bonnie's bones
- Picking up the map part at the end of a level

## What other items are there?
- **Bonnie Bones**: 15 of them are shuffled into the pool. Only required for the Bonnie goal.
- **Traps** (optional): Death, Ice, Flip, Blind, Relight and Confusion traps. Their frequency and individual weights can
  be tuned in the options.

## What is the goal?
There are 2 goals to pick from in the options:
- **Finish**: Beat the final boss. Reaching it requires all the other map parts.
- **Bonnie**: Give Bonnie back his bones. On top of beating the final boss, this requires all 15 bones to be in your
  inventory before reaching the end.

## Is DeathLink supported?
Yes. When enabled, an amnesty setting lets you choose how many of your own deaths it takes before a DeathLink is sent to
the other players.
